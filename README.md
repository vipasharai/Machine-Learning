# 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Collection

Welcome to my Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) repository! This space contains a series of hands-on data exploration, statistical analysis, and data visualization projects using Python.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools

* **Programming Language:** Python
* **Data Manipulation:** Pandas, NumPy
* **Data Visualization:** Matplotlib, Seaborn
* **Environment:** Jupyter Notebooks

---

## 📌 Featured Notebooks & Datasets

### 📊 HR Analytics (`EDA HR _Analytics.ipynb`)
* **Focus:** Employee attrition, department distributions, and workforce performance factors.
* **Key Insights:** Uncovering key metrics that influence employee retention and satisfaction.

### ✈️ Flight Dataset (`EDA_Flight Dataset.ipynb`)
* **Focus:** Flight ticket price variations, route efficiency, and delay patterns.
* **Key Visualizations:** Price distributions across airlines, peak travel times, and duration impacts.

### 🛂 US Visa Data (`EDA US Visa.ipynb`)
* **Focus:** Analysis of visa application status, wage distributions, and job title trends.
* **Key Insights:** Identifying patterns in approval rates across different employment categories.

### 🏥 Chronic Health Data (`EDA_Chronic Data Analysis.ipynb`)
* **Focus:** Medical and clinical parameter analysis across patient records.
* **Key Focus:** Identifying key correlation indicators and risk factors for health conditions.

### 🎓 Student Performance (`EDA_Student_Performanca.ipynb`)
* **Focus:** Factors impacting academic performance, including study habits and background.
* **Key Visualizations:** Feature distributions and correlation heatmaps of student score metrics.

### 🚴 Bike Details (`EDA BIKE DETAILS.ipynb`)
* **Focus:** Used bike market trends, pricing factors, and specifications.
* **Key Visualizations:** Price vs. mileage trends, brand distribution, and owner history impact.

### ✈️ Travel Dataset (`EDA_Travel_Dataset.ipynb`)
* **Focus:** Customer booking trends and travel package preferences.
* **Key Insights:** Customer demographic segmentation for targeted marketing strategies.
